Balanced Team Builder App Help

This web-hosted app helps you in building balanced teams of players based on their strengths and preferences.

Overview

The app reads player data from a file named players.txt and preferences from preferences.txt. Then it allows you to select players, specify the number of teams, and generates balanced team combinations.

Preferences between players are taken into account, giving a bonus to the strength of teams that include preferred pairs. Preferences represent additional strength because two players harmonize well together ("Robbery effect", i.e. two players who work exceptionally well together).

The app aims for the best balance in team strengths, considering preferences for bonuses.

Player Data

  • Players are loaded from players.txt, which is in CSV format: Player Name, Strength, Comment.
  • Strength is a value between 1 and 100.
  • You can adjust player strengths in the selection dialog. However, changes will not be saved back to the file

Preferences

  • Preferences are loaded from preferences.txt.
  • Also preferences are stored in CSV format: Player1, Player2, Bonus.
  • This file defines pairs of players who prefer to play together, with an additional strength bonus added if they are on the same team.
  • In solutions, these pairs are emphasized.

Using the App

  1. Load your own players.txt and preferences.txt or use the files from the web server.
  2. Select the players you want to include in team building.
  3. Specify the number of teams to build.
  4. The app will generate team combinations where the sum of strengths per team is as balanced as possible.
  5. Browse through different solutions. Permutations of previous solutions are not shown.
  6. Solutions where any team's strength sum differs by more than 10% from the average are suppressed.

Balancing Algorithm

For smaller groups of players (≤ 12), the algorithm exhaustively evaluates all possible non-empty partitions of the selected players into the required number of teams. For larger groups, it uses a randomized sampling search to find optimal solutions without freezing the browser. For each partition, it calculates the sum of player strengths and adds the synergy bonus for any preferred pairs that end up on the same team. It then calculates the "Strength Difference" (variance) across the teams. The solutions are sorted so the most balanced partitions appear first. Team compositions are internally stringified to automatically deduplicate solutions that are just permutations of one another.
